How it works
  • A beam leaves the emitter and travels in a straight line.
  • Tap a mirror to turn it. Each one bounces the beam ninety degrees.
  • Light every crystal to finish. Walls stop the beam dead.
  • Match par for three stars. There is no timer and no penalty for trying.
